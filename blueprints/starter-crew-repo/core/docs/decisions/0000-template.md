# NNNN: title stated as the outcome

- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Status:** proposal | accepted | working assumption | superseded by NNNN
- **Basis:** what the owner said, or which source supports it

## Decision

One to three sentences.

## Reason

Why this and not something else. Which options were dropped.

## Revisit when

The condition under which the decision must be checked again.
